RULES
  - Do not retype the wordmark. It is outlined here for a reason: the letter
    spacing is 0.40em and retyping it will not match.
  - Do not recolour anything. Use the version that matches the ground.
  - Do not stretch. Scale proportionally only.
  - Minimum size for the tile: 8mm. Below that the chevron closes up.
  - Clear space around the lockup: at least the height of the tile on all sides.

    deliberately not in this pack and should not be set under the mark.
